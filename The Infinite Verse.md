**The Infinite Verse 🌌**

**The Infinite Verse** is a cinematic, interactive web experience designed for relaxation, reflection, and poetic discovery. Set against a high-resolution ambient background, users can "spin" a digital reel to discover over a hundred original poems, complete with music, ambient animations, and special "Legendary" pulls.

**✨ Features**

- **Cinematic Entry:** A beautiful loading sequence with a poetic preamble and device selection (PC vs. Phone) to ensure the perfect layout and force fullscreen immersion.
- **The Poetry Reel:** A slot-machine-style spinning reel that shuffles through 128 original poems.
- **Rarity System:** 
- **The Blue Movement:** Standard, calming poems typed out on the screen.
- **The Purple Movement:** "Legendary" poems that trigger a custom particle explosion and feature a glowing gold/purple gradient text effect.
- **Ambient Animations:** Features roaming Lottie animations, including a dynamic blue butterfly that follows cursor clicks and randomly explores the screen.
- **Zen Mode:** An auto-play feature that automatically spins the reel and types out a new poem every 15 seconds.
- **Built-in Audio Player:** Integrated background music controls (Play/Pause, Next, Previous) to complete the relaxing atmosphere.

**🛠️ Tech Stack**

- **Frontend:** Vanilla HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript (ES6+).
- **Animations:** Custom CSS Keyframes and [Lottie (Bodymovin)](https://airbnb.io/lottie/) for SVG rendering.
- **Media:** Native HTML5 <video> and <audio> elements.
1. Open index.html in any modern web browser. (No local server is strictly required, though using one like VS Code's Live Server is recommended to prevent any strict browser CORS issues with local files).

**📱 Responsive Design**

The application features a hard layout switch based on the user's initial selection:

- **PC Mode:** A side-by-side split screen keeping the controls on the left and the poetry on the right.
- **Phone Mode:** A stacked vertical layout, moving the poetry to the top for visibility and keeping the controls at the bottom for easy thumb access.
